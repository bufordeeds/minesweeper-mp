How to play

Goal: together, reveal every square that isn't a mine. Numbers show how many mines touch that square. If anyone reveals a mine, the round is over for everyone — you win and lose as a team.

Controls

  • Reveal — tap / left-click a square
  • Flag a mine — long-press on touch, right-click with a mouse, or toggle 🚩 flag mode so every tap flags
  • Chord — tap a number that already has the right count of flags around it to reveal its remaining neighbors at once

Multiplayer

  • Everyone in the room shares one board — squares are tinted with the color of whoever revealed them, and you score a point per square.
  • Your first click of a fresh board is always safe.
  • Invite friends with 🔗 — anyone with the link joins your room.
